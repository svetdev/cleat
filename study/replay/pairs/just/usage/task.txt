The detailed help printed by `just --usage RECIPE` is hard to scan because the
entries sit almost flush with the section headings above them.
Every entry line listed under a section heading in `just --usage` output should
be indented by four spaces instead of the current two.
For example, with the justfile `foo bar:`, `just --usage foo` should print the
line `    bar` (four leading spaces) under `Arguments:`. The one-line usage
summary and the usage shown after an argument-count error should not change.
